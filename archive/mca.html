Masters of Computer Applications (MCA)

2-year postgraduate programme for science and commerce graduates — full-stack development, cloud computing, AI applications, and enterprise software engineering.

AICTE Approved  |  Intake: 60  |  2 Years (4 Semesters)  |  University of Mumbai Affiliated

About the Programme

The Masters of Computer Applications (MCA) at SLRTCE is a 2-year postgraduate programme open to graduates from any discipline who have studied Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level. It is the ideal pathway for non-engineering graduates who want to build a strong career in software development, IT management, cloud engineering, and applied AI.

The revised AICTE MCA curriculum integrates modern industry demands — covering full-stack web development, cloud platforms, mobile applications, data science, and cybersecurity — while retaining strong foundations in algorithms, databases, and software engineering principles.

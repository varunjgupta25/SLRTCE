Institute Overview

Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering (SLRTCE) is a premier autonomous engineering institution located at Kanakia Park, Mira Road (East), Mira Bhayandar — 401107, Mumbai, Maharashtra. Established under the Rahul Education Trust, the institute was founded with a singular vision: to create a world-class engineering education environment that is accessible, inclusive, and deeply connected to industry.

The institute is affiliated to the University of Mumbai, approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), and recognized by the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Maharashtra. SLRTCE holds autonomous status, enabling it to design and implement its own progressive curriculum that remains current with the rapidly evolving technology landscape.

Infrastructure & Facilities

The campus spans a modern multi-storey academic block equipped with technologically advanced infrastructure designed to support both theoretical instruction and hands-on practical learning:

  • State-of-the-art computer laboratories with high-speed internet connectivity
  • Advanced electronics, embedded systems, and communication engineering labs
  • Mechanical CAD/CAM/CAE and materials testing laboratories
  • Civil engineering surveying, geotechnical, and environmental labs
  • Centralized library with 30,000+ volumes, e-journal access, and digital repository
  • Smart classrooms with interactive projectors and IoT-enabled learning systems
  • Dedicated placement and career development center
  • e-yantra Robotics Lab (IIT Bombay collaboration)
  • Institution's Innovation Council (IIC) — MoE approved incubation center
  • Seminar halls and an auditorium with 500+ seating capacity
  • Sports facility, canteen, and student activity zones

Counselling Cell

Professional, confidential, and free counselling support for all SLRTCE students — academic guidance, career counselling, mental wellness, and personal development.

All counselling services at SLRTCE are completely confidential and free of charge for all enrolled students. Seeking help is a sign of strength, not weakness.

About the Counselling Cell

The Counselling Cell at Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering provides professional guidance and support to students navigating the academic, personal, and career challenges of engineering college life. The cell is staffed by trained counsellors and faculty mentors who work together to ensure every student feels heard, supported, and empowered.

The Counselling Cell operates in close collaboration with the Principal's Office, the Dean of Student Affairs, and department-level faculty mentors — creating a comprehensive support network across the institution.

Services Offered

ServiceDescription
Academic Counselling Guidance on study strategies, subject difficulties, exam stress, time management, and academic performance improvement. Available before and during examination periods.
Career Counselling One-on-one sessions to help students identify career goals, choose specializations, prepare for placements, and plan for higher education.
Personal Counselling Confidential support for students dealing with personal challenges — stress, anxiety, family issues, peer pressure, relationships, and identity concerns.
Mental Wellness Support Awareness sessions on mental health, stress management, mindfulness, and emotional resilience. Referrals to professional psychologists when required.
Mentor-Mentee Programme Every student is assigned a faculty mentor who conducts regular one-on-one meetings to track academic progress, address concerns, and provide guidance.
Anti-Ragging Support Zero-tolerance anti-ragging policy. Anonymous complaint mechanism and immediate response protocol for any ragging-related incidents.
Gender Sensitivity & ICC Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) handles all complaints related to sexual harassment. Gender sensitivity workshops conducted regularly.

Mentor-Mentee System

SLRTCE's structured Mentor-Mentee Programme assigns every student to a dedicated faculty mentor at the start of the academic year. Mentors meet with their mentees at least once per month to:

  • Monitor academic performance and attendance
  • Discuss personal and career goals
  • Address any concerns or difficulties the student is facing
  • Escalate serious issues to the Counselling Cell or Principal's Office
  • Provide guidance on internships, projects, and extracurricular activities

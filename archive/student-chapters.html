Professional Student Chapters

Connecting SLRTCE students to premier national and international engineering societies — providing access to technical publications, competitions, conferences, and industry networks.

Professional student chapters at SLRTCE bridge academic curriculum with the professional engineering world. By joining an accredited student chapter, engineering students earn recognized credentials, access technical symposiums, present peer-reviewed papers, and interface directly with industry leaders and researchers across India and abroad.

Recognized Student Chapters

CSI

CSI Student Chapter

Computer Society of India • Chapter I02352

The Computer Society of India student chapter at SLRTCE (I02352) organizes technical paper presentations, programming contests, research seminars, and industry expert interactions. Members receive CSI journals, discounts on conferences, and eligibility for state and national CSI student awards.

Key Offerings Professional Society

Conferences, research paper review mentorship, national hackathons, and certified computing workshops.

IETE

IETE Student Chapter

Institution of Electronics & Telecom Engineers (ISF)

The IETE Student Forum (ISF) provides a platform for Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering students to participate in national technical paper competitions, RF and wireless symposiums, project exhibitions, and IETE innovation grants.

Key Offerings Professional Society

Telecom industry mentorship, technical journal subscriptions, antenna and DSP seminars, and student paper awards.

ITSA

ITSA

Information Technology Student Association

The IT Student Association represents Information Technology undergraduates at SLRTCE — hosting departmental hackathons, tech talks on cloud computing, cybersecurity awareness, and hands-on coding bootcamps aligned with current industry needs.

Key Offerings Departmental Association

Annual IT fest, coding contests, technical workshops, and alumni mentorship programs.

IGS

IGS Student Chapter

Indian Geotechnical Society

Linked directly to the Department of Civil Engineering, the IGS Student Chapter engages students in geotechnical investigations, soil mechanics research, foundation design challenges, and site visits to major infrastructure and transit projects in the Mumbai metropolitan region.

Key Offerings Professional Society

Geotechnical site visits, structural modelling seminars, soil mechanics workshops, and national IGS student membership.

ECSSA

ECSSA

Electronics & Computer Science Student Association

The ECS Student Association represents students of Electronics & Computer Science engineering — organising robotics exhibitions, hardware-software co-design workshops, IoT bootcamps, and interdisciplinary technical challenges.

Key Offerings Departmental Association

Hands-on embedded systems workshops, drone tech exhibitions, VLSI introductory tracks, and project showcases.

Message from the COO

Dear Students, Parents, and Partners,

Running a modern engineering institution requires more than academic vision — it demands operational precision, financial discipline, and a relentless focus on the student experience. As Chief Operating Officer of Rahul Education Trust, my responsibility is to ensure that the infrastructure, systems, and processes of SLRTCE operate at the highest level of efficiency and quality.

Over the past several years, we have made strategic investments in digital transformation — upgrading our laboratories, expanding our library resources, deploying an enterprise ERP system for academic and administrative functions, and building a robust digital presence that keeps students, parents, and faculty connected at all times.

We have also made significant strides in our sustainability agenda — implementing energy-efficient systems, green campus initiatives, and waste management protocols that align with our responsibility to the environment and the community of Mira Road.

The operational backbone of SLRTCE — our admissions office, examination cell, placement center, finance department, and maintenance teams — works tirelessly behind the scenes to ensure that every student's experience at this institution is seamless, supportive, and exceptional. I am proud of every member of this team.

Chief Operating Officer
Rahul Education Trust — Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering

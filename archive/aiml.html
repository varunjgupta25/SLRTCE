About the Programme

The B.E. in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning is one of SLRTCE's newest and most in-demand programmes, launched in 2021 in response to the explosive growth in AI-driven industries. It is specifically designed for students who want to build, deploy, and optimize intelligent systems — from recommendation engines and autonomous vehicles to medical diagnostic tools and natural language interfaces.

Unlike a general computer science programme that touches on AI as one topic among many, AIML at SLRTCE places AI and ML at the centre of every semester. Students gain deep expertise in machine learning algorithms, neural network architectures, large language models, computer vision, and the engineering practices required to deploy AI systems at scale.

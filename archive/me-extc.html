M.E. Electronics & Telecommunication

Advanced postgraduate programme in Optical Communications, Microwave Systems, and AI-assisted Signal Processing. 18 intake, 2 years.

AICTE Approved  |  Intake: 18  |  2 Years Full-Time  |  Established: 2013

About the Programme

The M.E. in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering at SLRTCE is a rigorous postgraduate programme for engineers seeking mastery in advanced communication systems, photonics, microwave engineering, and next-generation wireless technologies. The programme combines advanced theory with experimental research and a full-year dissertation.

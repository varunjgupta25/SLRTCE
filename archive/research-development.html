The Department of R&D is focused to facilitate the SLRTCE research fraternity to execute their research activities in a seamless manner and showcase their research work through different competitions. The institution gives immense importance to training the students to generate technologically sound manpower.

Students and faculties conduct various research activities which include publications in international and national conferences and journals, book publications, patents, project competitions, idea competitions, and organization of conferences, workshops, and seminars in emerging areas of science and engineering.

To further strengthen our research structure, partnerships with industry and leading institutes are continually established. The Department of R&D also aims to inculcate the spirit of entrepreneurship in young minds and create a complete innovation ecosystem. I welcome you all to SLRTCE to be a part of our endeavor to offer you an excellent platform to actively participate in Research, Innovation and Invention activities while pursuing your BE, ME, and Ph.D. degrees.

Vision, Mission & Strategic Goals

Our Vision

To be recognized as a premier center of applied research and technological innovation, producing ethical researchers, patent holders, and entrepreneurs who address regional and global industrial challenges.

Our Mission

Cultivate an interdisciplinary research ecosystem by offering cutting-edge computing infrastructure, securing competitive research grants, establishing industry tie-ups, and mentoring faculty and students in intellectual property rights.

Incubation, IIC & Intellectual Property

The college hosts an active Institution's Innovation Council (IIC) approved by the Ministry of Education (MoE) Innovation Cell, a dedicated Startup Clinic, and a National Innovation and Startup Policy (NISP) implementation framework. The cell facilitates provisional patent drafting, prior art search, and technology transfer for student and faculty inventors.

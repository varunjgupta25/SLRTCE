Message from the Joint Secretary

Dear Students and Well-wishers,

It is a privilege to serve in the management of an institution that has established itself as a centre of engineering excellence in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region. Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering represents the best of what Indian technical education can be — rigorous, innovative, and deeply committed to student outcomes.

As Joint Secretary, my focus is on ensuring that the trust's vision translates into ground-level reality — that students experience the quality, infrastructure, and support that we promise. This means ongoing investment in our labs, libraries, and learning spaces; it means ensuring faculty receive the professional development they need to stay current; and it means building institutional systems that respond quickly and transparently to student needs.

I am particularly proud of the partnerships and collaborations SLRTCE has forged — with IIT Bombay, Skyline University Sharjah, the Ministry of Education's Innovation Council, and dozens of corporate recruiters. These relationships open real doors for our students, and expanding them further is one of my key priorities.

Joint Secretary
Rahul Education Trust — Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering

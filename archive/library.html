About the Library

The Central Library of Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering is the primary knowledge resource centre for all students, faculty, and research scholars. Spread across a spacious, well-lit reading facility, the library maintains an extensive collection of textbooks, reference books, journals, project reports, and digital resources covering all engineering and management disciplines.

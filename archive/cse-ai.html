About the Programme

The B.E. in CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Data Science) is a convergence programme that trains students to be both AI practitioners and data science professionals. Where pure AI programmes focus on model building and where pure DS programmes focus on data pipelines, this programme equips students with both skill sets — preparing them to build end-to-end intelligent data applications.

Graduates are equipped to design AI-powered products, build data-driven decision systems, and deploy scalable intelligent applications across domains including healthcare, finance, manufacturing, and smart cities.

About the Student Council

The Student Council of Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering serves as the official representative body of the student community. Working under the guidance of the Principal, Dean of Student Affairs, and Faculty Mentors, the Council bridges the dialogue between students and institutional leadership.

The Council oversees student representation, ensures campus welfare, and spearheads major inter-collegiate and intra-collegiate events, including Carpe Diem (Annual Fest), Technophilia (Technical Festival), and the Annual Sports Meet.

Key Objectives & Charter

  • Provide a structured forum for students to voice ideas, recommendations, and concerns to institute leadership.
  • Organize academic seminars, hackathons, and industry mentorship sessions in collaboration with professional chapters.
  • Promote social responsibility and civic engagement through blood donation drives, tree plantation, and DLLE voter awareness.
  • Maintain highest standards of academic integrity, campus discipline, and inclusive peer mentorship.

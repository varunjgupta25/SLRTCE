Student Life

SLRTCE is more than an engineering college — it is a community. Discover the clubs, fests, sports, social initiatives, and campus experiences that make student life here truly memorable.

Life at SLRTCE is a balance of rigorous academics and a rich campus culture — technical competitions, cultural festivals, sports, social service, and student-led clubs that fuel creativity, leadership, and lifelong friendships.

Campus Culture

The SLRTCE campus at Mira Road is a vibrant, inclusive environment where students from across Maharashtra and India come together. The college fosters a culture of academic excellence combined with holistic personal development. Whether you are passionate about coding, robotics, music, dance, sports, or social work — there is a space for you here.

Annual Highlights

EventTypeDescription
Carpe Diem Cultural Fest SLRTCE's flagship inter-collegiate cultural festival — dance, music, drama, art, and literary events. The 2025 edition saw SLRTCE win the championship trophy.
Technophilia Technical Fest Annual intra and inter-collegiate technical competition featuring hackathons, project exhibitions, robotics wars, and paper presentations.
Sports Meet Sports Annual sports competition covering cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, athletics, chess, and table tennis.
Alumni Meet Networking Annual gathering connecting current students with SLRTCE alumni for networking, career mentoring, and institutional nostalgia.
Freshers' Welcome Induction Formal welcome ceremony for new students, introducing them to college culture, faculty, and senior peers.

About the Programme

The B.E. in Computer Science & Engineering (Cyber Security) was launched in 2022 to address one of the most critical skill gaps in the global technology industry. Cybersecurity professionals are in extreme demand — India alone needs over 1 million cybersecurity specialists, and the global shortfall runs into millions. This programme trains students to become those specialists.

The curriculum covers both the offensive and defensive sides of cybersecurity — giving students the skills to think like attackers (ethical hacking, penetration testing) while building the expertise to defend systems, investigate incidents, and design secure architectures.

National Service Scheme (NSS)

"Not Me, But You" — the NSS unit at SLRTCE channels student energy into meaningful community service, social awareness, and national development activities.

"Not Me, But You" — the motto of NSS captures the spirit of selfless service that every volunteer embodies.

About NSS at SLRTCE

The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a Central Government programme under the Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports, aimed at developing the personality and character of students through voluntary community service. SLRTCE's NSS unit is an active and recognized unit under the University of Mumbai NSS Cell.

Through regular activities and an annual special camp, NSS volunteers at SLRTCE contribute to the communities around Mira Road — engaging in health awareness, environmental protection, digital literacy, and social welfare activities.

Objectives of NSS

  • Understand the community in which they work and live
  • Identify the needs and problems of the community and involve them in problem-solving
  • Develop among themselves a sense of social and civic responsibility
  • Utilise their knowledge in finding practical solutions to individual and community problems
  • Develop competence required for group-living and sharing of responsibilities
  • Gain skills in mobilising community participation
  • Acquire leadership qualities and democratic attitudes
  • Develop capacity to meet emergencies and natural disasters

How to Join

Any student enrolled in a B.E., MCA, or BMS programme at SLRTCE can register for the NSS unit at the start of the academic year. Registration forms are available at the Student Affairs office. NSS volunteers commit to a minimum of 120 hours of service per academic year.

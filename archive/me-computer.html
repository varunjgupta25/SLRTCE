About the Programme

The M.E. in Computer Engineering at SLRTCE is a research-focused postgraduate programme designed for engineers who want to deepen their technical expertise, pursue original research, and build careers in academia, R&D, and advanced engineering roles. The programme runs for 2 years (4 semesters) and culminates in a substantial research dissertation.

The curriculum blends advanced coursework with research seminars, journal publication guidance, and a thesis project supervised by experienced faculty with active research portfolios. Graduates are equipped for senior industry roles, doctoral programmes, and academic careers.

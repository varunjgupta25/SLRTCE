About the Career Development Cell

The Career Development Cell (CDC) at SLRTCE is the dedicated organizational unit that bridges the gap between the institution's talented graduates and the best employers in India and globally. The CDC operates year-round — working with students from their first year to build placement-ready profiles, and with recruiters to ensure a steady pipeline of campus hiring opportunities.

The CDC's approach goes beyond just conducting placement drives. It invests in students through continuous aptitude training, communication skill development, technical interview preparation, and live industry projects — ensuring that when a recruiter visits campus, every student is genuinely ready.

Core Values

The principles that define our academic culture, institutional character, and the way we develop every engineer at SLRTCE.

At Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering, our core values are not aspirational statements — they are the lived principles that govern every academic, administrative, and student interaction at the institute.

Our Core Values

Academic Excellence

We set high academic standards and continuously invest in faculty development, curriculum upgrades, and outcome-based education to ensure every student receives a world-class engineering education.

Integrity & Ethics

We uphold the highest standards of honesty, fairness, and professional ethics — in the classroom, in research, and in every interaction with students, faculty, and the wider community.

Transparency

From admissions and fee structures to evaluations and placements, SLRTCE ensures complete transparency so that students and parents have access to all information needed to make informed decisions.

Innovation & Research

We foster a culture of curiosity, critical thinking, and creative problem-solving through funded research programs, patent support, industry collaborations, and an active Innovation Council.

Inclusivity & Diversity

We are committed to creating an equal and inclusive campus where students from all backgrounds, geographies, and economic strata have access to quality education, scholarships, and career support.

Social Responsibility

Through NSS, DLLE, the Electoral Literacy Club, and community outreach programs, we instil a sense of civic duty and national responsibility in every student, preparing them to be ethical citizens.

Student-Centric Approach

Every decision — academic, infrastructural, or administrative — is driven by what is best for the student. Faculty accessibility, student counselling, and placement support are integral to our identity.

Continuous Improvement

We believe that excellence is a process, not a destination. Our ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management system ensures that we continuously evaluate, refine, and improve every aspect of institutional performance.

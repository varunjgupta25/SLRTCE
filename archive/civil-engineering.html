About the Department

The Department of Civil Engineering at SLRTCE offers a B.E. in Civil Engineering with Computer Application — a forward-looking programme that integrates classical civil engineering with digital tools, smart infrastructure design, and data-driven construction management. The curriculum prepares graduates for careers in construction, infrastructure development, urban planning, structural design, and government engineering services.

With India's massive investment in infrastructure — roads, bridges, metros, smart cities, and affordable housing — civil engineering graduates with digital skills are among the most sought-after professionals in the country.

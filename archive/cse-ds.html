AICTE Approved  |  B.E. Intake: 60  |  Established: 2021  |  CSE Specialization

About the Programme

The B.E. in Computer Science & Engineering (Data Science) is a specialization programme that combines a strong computer science foundation with deep expertise in data engineering, analytics, and business intelligence. The programme prepares students to work as data engineers, analysts, and scientists who can collect, process, model, and communicate insights from large and complex datasets.

Data Science graduates are among the most sought-after professionals globally, with demand spanning every industry — from finance and healthcare to retail, manufacturing, and government.

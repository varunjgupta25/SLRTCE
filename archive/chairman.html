Message from the Chairman

Dear Students, Parents, and Members of the Academic Community,

It is with great pride and a deep sense of purpose that I extend this message to you from Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering. Since the founding of the Rahul Education Trust, our singular mission has been to democratize quality education — to make world-class engineering and management education accessible to every deserving student, regardless of their economic background or geographic location.

SLRTCE was established as a living embodiment of that mission. Over the years, we have grown from a modest engineering college into a fully autonomous, NAAC and NBA-accredited institution with hundreds of industry partnerships, a growing research cell, and a placement record that reflects the real value of the education we deliver.

The challenges of the 21st century demand engineers who are not just technically competent, but adaptable, ethical, and globally aware. Our autonomous status allows us to build exactly such a curriculum — one that stays ahead of industry trends, incorporates emerging technologies like AI, ML, cybersecurity, and sustainable engineering, and produces graduates ready for both corporate excellence and entrepreneurial endeavors.

I am deeply grateful to our dedicated faculty, supportive administrative staff, and most importantly, to our students — whose achievements are the greatest validation of everything we do at SLRTCE.

Chairman
Rahul Education Trust — Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering

About Rahul Education Trust

Rahul Education Trust is the governing body that oversees the management of Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering and its associated institutions. The Trust is committed to promoting technical and professional education across Maharashtra with a focus on quality, accessibility, and innovation.

About the Research Centre

Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering is a recognized Ph.D. research centre under the University of Mumbai for Computer Engineering and Information Technology. The R&D Cell and Ph.D. programme work together to create a vibrant research ecosystem — supporting doctoral scholars with high-end computing infrastructure, patent filing assistance, conference and journal publication funding, and mentorship from experienced faculty researchers.

Ph.D. scholars at SLRTCE benefit from active industry collaborations, IIT Bombay research tie-ups, and an institutional culture that encourages original, impactful research in emerging technology domains.

Eligibility & Admission Process

StepRequirement
Qualifying DegreeM.E./M.Tech or M.Sc. in relevant subject with minimum 55% aggregate
Entrance TestUniversity of Mumbai Ph.D. Entrance Test (PET) — OR — NET / SET / GATE qualified (exempt from PET)
Research ProposalSubmission of a 2–3 page research proposal outlining the proposed area of study
InterviewPresentation before the Research Recognition Committee (RRC) at University of Mumbai
Supervisor AllocationMatched with an approved faculty guide based on research area alignment
RegistrationFormal Ph.D. registration at University of Mumbai after RRC approval

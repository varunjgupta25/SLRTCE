Message from the Principal

Dear Students, Parents, Faculty, and Well-wishers,

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering — an institution that has consistently stood as a beacon of quality engineering education in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region. Since its establishment, SLRTCE has evolved from a single-campus engineering college into a multi-disciplinary, autonomous institution with a proud legacy of academic achievement, research innovation, and career excellence.

Our institute operates on the foundational belief that true education transcends the boundaries of a classroom. We invest not just in curriculum delivery, but in the holistic development of every student who walks through our doors. Through our NBA-accredited programmes in Computer Engineering and Information Technology, our nationally recognized research cell, our active Industry–Institute Interaction Cell, and our dedicated Placement and Career Development Center, we ensure that every graduate leaves SLRTCE equipped to succeed in a competitive, technology-driven world.

As an autonomous institution under the University of Mumbai, we have the unique privilege and responsibility of crafting our own academic programmes. This autonomy allows us to stay ahead of the curve — incorporating AI, machine learning, cloud computing, cybersecurity, and sustainable engineering into our curricula before these become standard in conventional programmes. It is a responsibility we take very seriously.

I invite every aspiring engineer to come and experience SLRTCE — not just as a college, but as a launchpad for your career and a community that will support your growth at every step. Our doors are open, our faculty is dedicated, and our commitment to your success is unwavering.

Together, let us build a future worthy of the world we wish to create.

Dr. Umesh S. Bhadade
Principal, Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering

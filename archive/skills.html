Skills @ SLRTCE

Industry MoUs, national lab partnerships, and skill development collaborations that give SLRTCE students a competitive edge beyond the classroom.

SLRTCE's skill development ecosystem goes beyond the classroom — through MoU partnerships with IITs, international universities, government bodies, and industry leaders, students gain access to certifications, labs, and real-world exposure unavailable in most institutions.

Admission & Eligibility Criteria

Detailed eligibility requirements for all programmes offered at Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering for AY 2026-27.

All eligibility criteria are as per DTE Maharashtra, AICTE, and University of Mumbai regulations for AY 2026-27. Candidates must verify the latest DTE circulars for any revisions.

First Year B.E. / B.Tech

CriterionRequirement
Qualifying ExaminationHSC (Class 12) or equivalent with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics (PCM)
Minimum Marks (General)45% aggregate in PCM subjects
Minimum Marks (SC/ST/OBC)40% aggregate in PCM subjects
Entrance ExamMHT-CET (Maharashtra) or JEE Main score mandatory
AgeNo upper age limit as per AICTE norms
DomicileMaharashtra State eligibility required for state quota seats

Direct Second Year B.E. (DSE / Lateral Entry)

CriterionRequirement
Qualifying Examination3-year Diploma in Engineering (relevant branch) OR B.Sc. with Mathematics
Minimum Marks (General)45% aggregate in qualifying examination
Minimum Marks (SC/ST)40% aggregate
Entrance ExamMAH-Diploma CET (for diploma holders) or applicable CET score
Branch RelevanceDiploma branch must be relevant to the B.E. branch being sought

M.E. / M.Tech (Postgraduate)

CriterionRequirement
Qualifying ExaminationB.E. / B.Tech in relevant engineering branch
Minimum Marks (General)50% aggregate in B.E. / B.Tech
Minimum Marks (SC/ST)45% aggregate
Entrance ExamMAH-M.E./M.Tech CET or valid GATE score
Programmes AvailableM.E. Computer Engineering, M.E. Electronics & Telecommunication

MCA (Masters of Computer Applications)

CriterionRequirement
Qualifying ExaminationBachelor's degree in any discipline with Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level
Minimum Marks50% aggregate (45% for SC/ST)
Entrance ExamMAH-MCA CET

BMS / BSc Management

CriterionRequirement
Qualifying ExaminationHSC (Class 12) any stream
Minimum Marks45% aggregate (40% for SC/ST)
Admission BasisMerit-based / CET as applicable

Ph.D. Research Programme

CriterionRequirement
Qualifying ExaminationM.E. / M.Tech or M.Sc. in relevant subject with minimum 55% aggregate
Entrance TestUniversity of Mumbai Ph.D. Entrance Test (PET) or NET/SET/GATE qualified
InterviewResearch proposal presentation and interview before the Research Recognition Committee
Disciplines AvailableComputer Engineering, Information Technology

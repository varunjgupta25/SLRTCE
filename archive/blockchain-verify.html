How Verification Works

StepAction
1The graduate shares their blockchain transcript (PDF or digital link) with the employer or institution
2The verifier locates the Verification Code or Document Hash printed on the transcript
3Enter the code in the verification portal above and click Verify
4The system queries the blockchain and returns the verified credential data in real-time
5A verified result confirms the document is genuine and unaltered since issuance

Why Blockchain?

FeatureBenefit
ImmutabilityOnce recorded on the blockchain, credentials cannot be altered or deleted
DecentralizationNo single point of failure — the record exists across multiple nodes
Instant VerificationVerification takes seconds — no waiting for college responses or physical document couriering
Global AccessibilityAny employer or institution worldwide can verify credentials online
Fraud PreventionEliminates fake degree certificates and transcript forgery

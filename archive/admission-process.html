Admission Process

Step-by-step guide to securing admission at SLRTCE for B.E., M.E., MCA, BMS, and Ph.D. programmes for Academic Year 2026-27.

Admissions Open for AY 2026-27. For immediate counselling call +91 (022) 2812 0144 / 45 (Mon–Sat, 9 AM–5 PM) or submit an online enquiry.

First Year B.E. / B.Tech Admission (CAP Round)

First Year B.E. admissions are conducted through the Centralized Admission Process (CAP) administered by the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Maharashtra.

  • 1

    Appear in MHT-CET / JEE Main

    Qualify Maharashtra State CET (MHT-CET) or JEE Main with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. Score is the primary merit basis.

  • 2

    Register on DTE Maharashtra Portal

    Visit fe2025.mahacet.org and complete the online CAP registration with personal, academic, and document details.

  • 3

    Document Verification (ARC)

    Visit your nearest Admission Reporting Centre (ARC) with originals: SSC, HSC marksheets, CET scorecard, domicile certificate, caste certificate (if applicable), and Aadhaar.

  • 4

    Fill Option Form

    After document verification, fill the online option form on the DTE portal — select SLRTCE (EN3218) and preferred branches in order of preference.

  • 5

    Seat Allotment

    DTE publishes provisional and final merit lists. Accept allotted seat online within the deadline and pay the seat acceptance fee.

  • 6

    Report to SLRTCE

    Report to the college admission office with original documents and fee payment receipt. Complete institute-level registration and enrolment formalities.

Direct Second Year (DSE) Admission

Diploma holders and B.Sc. graduates can seek lateral entry into the Second Year B.E. through DSE CAP rounds conducted by DTE Maharashtra.

  • 1

    Eligibility Check

    Diploma in relevant engineering branch (min. 45% aggregate) or B.Sc. with Mathematics (min. 45%). SC/ST candidates: 40%.

  • 2

    Register on DTE DSE Portal

    Complete DSE CAP registration on the DTE Maharashtra portal with diploma/B.Sc. marksheets and required documents.

  • 3

    Merit List & Allotment

    Allotment based on diploma/B.Sc. percentage. Fill option form selecting SLRTCE branches of preference.

  • 4

    Report to College

    Complete document verification and fee payment at the SLRTCE admissions office.

M.E. / M.Tech Admission

  • 1

    Qualify PGET / GATE

    Appear in Maharashtra PG Engineering CET (MAH-M.E./M.Tech CET) or hold a valid GATE score.

  • 2

    CAP Registration & Option Form

    Register on DTE portal, fill option form selecting M.E. Computer Engineering or M.E. EXTC at SLRTCE.

  • 3

    Allotment & Reporting

    Accept allotment and report to SLRTCE with B.E./B.Tech marksheets, PGET/GATE scorecard, and required documents.

Vision & Mission

Our guiding principles, institutional philosophy, and the programme outcomes that shape every graduate of Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering.

"To be a globally recognized institution of engineering and technology, nurturing creative and ethical professionals who contribute to sustainable societal development."

Institute Vision — SLRTCE

Institute Vision

To be a globally recognized institution of engineering and technology, nurturing creative, ethical, and technically proficient professionals who contribute meaningfully to sustainable national and societal development.

Institute Mission

  • Provide quality technical education through an outcome-based, industry-aligned curriculum that develops analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Foster an environment of academic excellence, applied research, and continuous innovation through state-of-the-art infrastructure and faculty development.
  • Instil professional ethics, integrity, and a sense of social responsibility in every student to produce responsible engineers and leaders.
  • Build strong industry partnerships, MoU collaborations, and research tie-ups that translate into real-world opportunities for students and faculty.
  • Promote inclusive education, diversity, and student welfare through counselling, scholarships, and equal-opportunity programmes.

Quality Policy

SLRTCE is committed to providing a quality learning environment that continuously improves through ISO 9001:2015 certified processes, faculty upgradation, infrastructure investment, and student-centric governance. Every academic and administrative decision is guided by the principles of transparency, accountability, and measurable student outcomes.

Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs)

Graduates of SLRTCE engineering programmes shall:

# Programme Educational Objective
PEO 1 Apply fundamental engineering knowledge and modern tools to design, analyze, and solve complex real-world technical problems.
PEO 2 Demonstrate professional competence and ethical responsibility in engineering practice, research, and industry environments.
PEO 3 Pursue lifelong learning through higher education, certifications, and self-directed professional development in evolving technology domains.
PEO 4 Effectively communicate technical ideas, collaborate in multidisciplinary teams, and contribute to organizational and societal goals.
PEO 5 Contribute to entrepreneurship, innovation, and sustainable technological solutions that address local and global challenges.

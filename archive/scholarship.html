Scholarships & Financial Aid

All government, institutional, and merit-based scholarship and freeship schemes available to students at SLRTCE.

SLRTCE is committed to ensuring that financial constraints never stand between a deserving student and a quality engineering education. Multiple government and institutional financial aid schemes are available.

Government of Maharashtra Schemes

SchemeBenefitEligible CategoryPortal
MahaDBT — Post-Matric Scholarship Full tuition fee + maintenance allowance reimbursement SC, ST, OBC, NT, SBC, EWS students mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in
Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS) 100% tuition fee waiver at the time of admission All categories — merit-based; annual family income ≤ ₹ 8 Lakh DTE Maharashtra CAP Portal
EBC Freeship Full tuition fee exemption Open category; annual family income ≤ ₹ 8 Lakh mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in
Minority Scholarship Tuition fee + maintenance allowance Muslim, Christian, Buddhist, Parsi, Sikh, Jain minorities mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in
OBC / NT / SBC Scholarship Tuition fee + maintenance allowance OBC, NT-A, NT-B, NT-C, NT-D, SBC categories mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in

Central Government Schemes

SchemeBenefitEligible Category
GOI Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST Full tuition + maintenance allowance Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe students
Central Sector Scheme of Scholarships ₹ 10,000 per year for graduation; ₹ 20,000 for PG Top 20 percentile in HSC; family income ≤ ₹ 8 Lakh
Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme (PMSS) Up to ₹ 25,000 per year Wards of ex-servicemen and ex-coast guard personnel
National Scholarship Portal (NSP) Multiple central schemes under one portal As per individual scheme criteria — scholarships.gov.in

How to Apply

StepAction
1Secure admission to SLRTCE through DTE CAP rounds
2Identify applicable scheme based on your category and income
3Register on mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in or scholarships.gov.in
4Submit application with required documents: Aadhaar, income certificate, caste certificate, marksheets, bank passbook
5College scholarship cell verifies and forwards application to government
6Scholarship amount credited directly to student's bank account (DBT)

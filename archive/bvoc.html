Bachelor of Vocation (B.Voc)

Industry-integrated vocational degree programmes in Cybersecurity & Digital Forensics and Big Database & Cloud Computing — building job-ready professionals.

B.Voc is a 3-year vocational degree recognized by UGC, designed to produce job-ready graduates with both academic knowledge and industry-certified practical skills. Multiple exit options available after Year 1 (Certificate) and Year 2 (Diploma).

About B.Voc

The Bachelor of Vocation (B.Voc) is a unique degree framework introduced by the University Grants Commission (UGC) under the National Skill Qualification Framework (NSQF). It combines academic education with structured vocational training, ensuring that graduates are immediately employable in their chosen technical domains.

SLRTCE offers two B.Voc specializations — both aligned with the highest-demand technology domains in India's current job market.

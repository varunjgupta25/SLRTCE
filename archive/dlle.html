Department of Lifelong Learning & Extension (DLLE)

Extending the impact of education beyond the campus — DLLE volunteers at SLRTCE work on voter awareness, women empowerment, adult literacy, and community development.

DLLE is a University of Mumbai initiative that trains students to become agents of social change — taking the skills and awareness they develop in college out into the community to create lasting impact.

About DLLE

The Department of Lifelong Learning and Extension (DLLE) is an extension wing of the University of Mumbai, established to involve students in adult education and community service through structured extension activities. SLRTCE has an active DLLE unit that regularly participates in University-assigned extension projects and social initiatives.

DLLE volunteers earn credits and certificates recognized by the University of Mumbai. The programme develops communication, leadership, community engagement, and civic responsibility in students — skills that complement their technical education.

DLLE Schemes & Activities

Scheme / ActivityDescription
Voter Registration Drive (ELC) In collaboration with the Election Commission's Electoral Literacy Club — helping students and community members register as first-time voters. SLRTCE conducted a voter registration drive with students displaying their new voter ID cards.
Adult & Non-Formal Education Volunteers teach basic reading, writing, and arithmetic to adults in nearby communities who lack access to formal education.
Women Empowerment Programme Workshops on financial literacy, self-defence awareness, health and hygiene, and digital safety for women in nearby communities.
Consumer Awareness Educating community members about consumer rights, digital payment safety, and protection against fraud.
Health & Hygiene Awareness Campaigns on sanitation, menstrual health, nutrition, and preventive healthcare in the Mira Road community.
Environment Awareness Programmes on waste segregation, plastic-free living, water conservation, and sustainable practices.

How to Join

Students in B.E. First or Second Year can register for DLLE at the beginning of the academic year through the Student Affairs Office. Participation requires commitment to a minimum of 120 hours of approved extension activities.

Message from the Secretary

Dear Students, Parents, and Academic Community,

The role of the Secretary in an educational trust goes far beyond administrative formalities — it is about ensuring that every policy, every process, and every decision is aligned with the institution's core commitment to student welfare and academic excellence.

At SLRTCE, we operate under a robust governance framework that ensures complete transparency from the moment a student applies, through their four years of academic life, and into their placement and alumni journey. Our mandatory disclosure practices, grievance redressal mechanism, anti-ragging policies, and Internal Complaints Committee are not just regulatory requirements — they are active, functional systems that protect and support every member of our community.

We have invested significantly in upgrading the physical and digital infrastructure of the college — from smart classrooms and high-speed internet labs to an active online portal for students and parents. These investments reflect our belief that administrative efficiency is a direct enabler of academic excellence.

I look forward to welcoming every new batch of students to SLRTCE and assure you that the trust and its management are fully committed to making your experience at this institution the most impactful years of your life.

Secretary
Rahul Education Trust — Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering

Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM)

2-year management programme with specializations in Finance, Business Analytics, Marketing, Corporate Law, Cyber Law, and Management.

Intake: 60  |  2 Years (4 Semesters)  |  Multiple Specializations  |  Industry-Integrated Curriculum

About the Programme

The Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) at SLRTCE is a 2-year full-time management programme that combines strong business fundamentals with specialized knowledge tracks tailored to current industry demand. The programme is open to graduates from any discipline and is designed to produce professionals with both strategic thinking and domain expertise.

What sets SLRTCE's PGDM apart is its technology-management convergence — students graduating from engineering backgrounds find a natural bridge between their technical skills and business leadership, while non-engineering graduates gain exposure to the technology dimensions reshaping every industry.

Message from the President

Dear Students, Faculty, and Stakeholders,

As President of Rahul Education Trust, I have had the privilege of witnessing SLRTCE's remarkable journey — from its early years to its current status as one of Mumbai's most respected autonomous engineering institutions. This transformation has been driven by one constant: our commitment to student success.

The world of engineering and technology is changing at an unprecedented pace. Artificial intelligence, green energy, cybersecurity, and biotechnology are reshaping every industry. SLRTCE has responded by investing heavily in modern infrastructure, hiring expert faculty, forging international academic partnerships, and building a curriculum that prepares students not just for the jobs of today, but for the careers of tomorrow.

Our placement record, our research output, and the achievements of our alumni across India and the world speak louder than any brochure. I encourage every student who joins SLRTCE to take full advantage of the opportunities on offer — internships, research projects, club activities, competitions, and the mentorship of our outstanding faculty.

The institute's doors are always open to questions, ideas, and feedback. We grow when our students grow, and that is the commitment we make to every family that entrusts their child's future to us.

President
Rahul Education Trust — Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering
